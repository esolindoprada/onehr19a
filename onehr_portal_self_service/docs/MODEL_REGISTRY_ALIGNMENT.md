
# OneHR Portal Self-Service — Model Registry Alignment

The supplied OneHR Portal product baseline explicitly names the three Portal business models:
- `onehr.portal.request`
- `onehr.portal.request.type`
- `onehr.portal.request.stage`

This build implements exactly those three persistent Portal business models and extends only
native/shared `res.users` and `hr.employee`. Two UI helpers are transient models.
