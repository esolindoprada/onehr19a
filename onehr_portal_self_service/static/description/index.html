OneHR Portal Self-Service

My HR • My Team • HR Requests • Approvals • SLA

Enterprise employee and manager self-service for OneHR on Odoo 19 Community Edition. Employees submit and follow governed HR requests; managers act on direct-team approvals; HR controls service catalogues, SLA, final decisions and secure downstream hand-offs.

Employee & Manager Experience

  • My HR dashboard and request history.
  • Save Draft, Submit, Cancel, comments and attachments.
  • My Team direct-manager approval queue.
  • Clear request state, SLA and downstream status.

Enterprise Governance

  • Manager/HR approval evidence and immutable submitted history.
  • Company isolation and least-privilege model authority.
  • Configurable SLA/reminder monitoring.
  • Security-preserving soft integration with wider OneHR domains.
