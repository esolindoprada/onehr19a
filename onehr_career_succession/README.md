# OneHR Career & Succession

Enterprise Career & Succession Management for OneHR NEW on Odoo 19 Community Edition.

The addon implements exactly the product-approved four persistent business models:

- `onehr.competency`
- `onehr.position.competency`
- `onehr.talent.pool`
- `onehr.succession.plan`

Hard dependencies remain exactly `onehr_base` and `onehr_corehr`.
Appraisal, Learning and Analytics are soft/downstream integrations.

Main capabilities:
- competency dictionary with level scale and behavioural evidence;
- position competency requirements and gap-oriented analysis;
- high-potential talent pool with performance, potential, learning and readiness scores;
- 9-box positioning and succession readiness;
- succession planning for critical positions with multiple successor candidates;
- bench-strength, coverage and position-risk analytics;
- native Odoo CSV import support for competency master;
- governed soft integration services for Appraisal, Learning, Analytics, Portal and API;
- enterprise List/Form/Search/Kanban/Pivot/Graph UI;
- PDF competency-gap and succession-plan reports;
- audit trail, company isolation and evidence-preservation controls.

Source acceptance is a prerequisite only. Windows/Odoo runtime acceptance remains mandatory.
