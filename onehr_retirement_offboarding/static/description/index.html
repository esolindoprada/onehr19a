OneHR — Retirement & Offboarding

Resign/Retire • Checklist Handover • Exit Interview • Revoke Akses • Final Benefit

Modul offboarding terpadu untuk mengelola pengunduran diri & pensiun karyawan: buat kasus offboarding, hasilkan checklist clearance lintas fungsi (HR/IT/Finance), lakukan exit interview, cabut akses, dan siapkan final payout/benefit— terintegrasi dengan ekosistem OneHR pada Odoo 18 Community Edition.

Odoo 18 CE Multi-company/Branch Workflow Approval Portal Self-Service Mail & Activities Attrition Analytics

Overview

OneHR — Retirement & Offboarding memusatkan proses akhir masa kerja karyawan: dari permintaan offboarding, penugasan checklist per pemilik tugas (HR/IT/Finance/Line Manager), penjadwalan exit interview, revokasi akses sistem/SSO, hingga rekap clearance & pengukuran KPI. Semua berlangsung dengan approval matrix, pengingat SLA, dan dukungan multi-company/branch.

Key Features

  • Offboarding Case dengan tahapan Requested → In Progress → Completed → Closed
  • Checklist Template per job family/grade; auto-generate tugas clearance
  • Ownership tugas lintas fungsi (HR/IT/Finance/Line Manager) + SLA & reminder
  • Asset & Clearance: daftar item (laptop, kartu akses, email alias, dll.) & status pengembalian
  • Exit Interview (pertanyaan, skor, alasan keluar) + analitik attrition
  • Revoke Access: penonaktifan users Odoo & trigger SSO (via integrasi)
  • Final Benefit: jembatan ke payroll/finance untuk perhitungan final payout
  • Mail/Activities: undangan, reminder tugas, & notifikasi kelulusan clearance
  • Portal: karyawan melihat status offboarding & mengisi exit interview
  • Audit & Compliance: jejak aktivitas, siapa mencabut apa & kapan

Core Models

  • onehr.offboarding — master kasus offboarding (karyawan, tanggal, state, SLA)
  • onehr.offboarding.checklist — tugas clearance per owner (HR/IT/Finance/Manager)
  • onehr.clearance.item — katalog item serah-terima (asset, credential, akses)
  • onehr.exit.interview — form pertanyaan, skor, & alasan keluar
  • hr.employee (inherit) — exit_date, exit_reason, tombol smart Offboarding
  • hr.contract (inherit) — keterkaitan terminasi & final payout
  • res.users (inherit) — flag/referensi revokasi akses

Pelaporan: time-to-close, tugas terlambat, distribusi alasan keluar.

Workflow Ringkas

  1. HR/Manager membuat Offboarding (resign/retire), tetapkan tanggal & alasan.
  2. Generate Checklist dari template → tugas dibagikan ke HR/IT/Finance/Manager.
  3. Jadwalkan Exit Interview; karyawan mengisi kuesioner (portal).
  4. Revoke Access & penarikan aset (kartu, laptop, email, SSO, dsb.).
  5. Proses Final Benefit/Payout (bridge ke payroll/finance).
  6. Semua tugas Completed → kasus Closed; dokumen & audit tersimpan.

Holistic Integrations (OneHR Suite)

Terintegrasi dengan: onehr_corehr, onehr_security, onehr_workflow_approval, onehr_document (lampiran & form), onehr_portal_self_service (akses karyawan), onehr_payroll_bridge (final payout), serta modul analytics/reporting OneHR. Integrasi bersifat soft-coupled via relasi, server action, mail template, dan event log.

Installation

  1. Tambahkan modul ke addons_path Odoo.
  2. Update Apps list, cari OneHR — Retirement & Offboarding.
  3. Install; dependensi inti akan terpasang otomatis.

Configuration

  • Atur Checklist Templates per job family/grade & owner tugas.
  • Tentukan Approval Matrix, SLA & reminder (cron/activities).
  • Siapkan Exit Interview Q&A & kategori alasan keluar.
  • Aktifkan bridges: document, payroll, & revoke SSO bila diperlukan.

Security

  • Groups: HR Manager, HR Officer, IT Stakeholder, Finance Stakeholder.
  • Record Rules: scope perusahaan/cabang & kepemilikan tugas.
  • Audit: pencatatan aksi revokasi & clearance untuk keperluan compliance.
