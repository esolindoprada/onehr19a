OneHR — Analytics & KPI Dashboards

Enterprise KPI governance and immutable trend evidence for Odoo 19 Community Edition

Centralize workforce, recruitment, attendance/time-off, learning, payroll, performance/succession and compliance KPI monitoring without moving ownership away from the source OneHR addons.

Enterprise capabilities

  • Governed KPI Tiles with target, value format and fixed provider modes.
  • Daily / weekly / monthly immutable snapshots with previous-period, MoM, HoH and YoY trend evidence.
  • Warning / critical thresholds with de-duplicated Odoo activities.
  • Drill-through under the current user's normal source-domain access.
  • Company isolation and soft branch fail-closed behavior.
  • Executive Kanban, enterprise List/Form/Search, Pivot and Graph analysis.
  • CSV snapshot export for external BI pipelines.
  • User KPI favorites without creating a separate dashboard/favorite business table.

Approved model footprint

  • onehr.analytics.tile — KPI definition, lifecycle, targets, thresholds, schedule and latest cache.
  • onehr.kpi.snapshot — immutable periodic KPI evidence.
  • onehr.mixin.analytics — abstract helper for periods, thresholds, literal domains and trend calculations.

No employee, applicant, attendance, leave, payroll, learning, appraisal, succession, discipline, API-authentication or branch master is duplicated.

Soft-coupled OneHR providers

The approved model-aggregate provider supports a bounded allow-list of OneHR domains. Explicit service contracts are consumed for Appraisal (onehr_appraisal_kpi_snapshot), Learning (onehr_learning_kpi_snapshot) and Career/Succession (onehr_career_kpi_snapshot) when installed. Source ACLs and record rules remain authoritative and no business sudo is used.

Public REST/API authentication remains owned by onehr_integrations_api. This addon exposes only a narrow model-service KPI feed for downstream transport.

Security

Analytics Analyst, Analytics Manager and Analytics Administrator are independent least-privilege roles with global company rules.

Evidence

Snapshots cannot be edited or deleted through the application. Historical evidence is archived, not automatically removed.

Community Edition

No Enterprise-only Spreadsheet, Cohort or Gantt renderer is fabricated. CSV and native Pivot/Graph are the supported analysis/export surfaces in this build.
