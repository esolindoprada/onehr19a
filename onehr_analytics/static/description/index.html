OneHR — Analytics & KPI Dashboards

Headcount • Turnover • Time-to-Hire • Absenteeism • Payroll Cost • Learning KPIs

Pusat analitik SDM untuk memantau KPI, menyimpan snapshot periodik, menampilkan dashboard eksekutif, dan mengirim alert otomatis — terintegrasi dengan seluruh ekosistem OneHR di Odoo 18 Community Edition.

Odoo 18 CE Multi-company/Branch Pivot/Graph/Spreadsheet Scheduler Snapshot Alert & Threshold

Overview

Modul ini menyatukan pengukuran kinerja HR: KPI tiles yang dapat dikonfigurasi, snapshot berkala untuk tren historis, pivot/graph/cohort untuk eksplorasi data, serta alert ambang untuk eskalasi cepat. Integrasi mencakup Rekrutmen, Kehadiran, Cuti, Payroll, Pembelajaran, Appraisal, dan Disiplin.

Key Features

  • KPI Tiles bisa dikonfigurasi (rumus, filter, warna, target, format).
  • Snapshots via cron (harian/mingguan/bulanan) untuk tren dan YoY/HoH/MoM.
  • Alert & Threshold (Email/Activities) saat KPI melewati batas.
  • Drill-through dari tile ke daftar data sumber yang relevan.
  • Multi-company/branch dengan filter konteks & record rules.
  • Pivot/Graph/Cohort & ekspor CSV/XLSX untuk BI eksternal.
  • Bridges: Attendance, Timeoff, Recruitment, Learning, Payroll, Appraisal, Discipline.
  • Dashboard eksekutif siap pakai + favorit per user.
  • Spreadsheet sync untuk laporan dinamis & bagikan ke tim.

Core Models

  • onehr.analytics.tile — definisi kartu KPI (rumus, target, visual).
  • onehr.kpi.snapshot — penyimpanan nilai KPI periodik.
  • onehr.mixin.analytics — util agregasi, threshold, dan alert.

Dataset dibangun dari model lintas modul OneHR dengan pendekatan soft-coupled agar addon bisa diinstal independen namun tetap terintegrasi.

Typical HR KPIs

Workforce: Headcount, New Hire, Attrition/Turnover, Tenure Distribution, Diversity Mix.
Recruitment: Time-to-Hire, Time-to-Fill, Offer Acceptance Rate, Funnel Conversion.
Attendance: Absenteeism Rate, Overtime Hours, Punctuality, Leave Utilization.
Learning: Training Hours/FTE, Completion Rate, Certification Status.
Payroll & Comp: Payroll Cost/FTE, Comp Ratio, Overtime Cost, Allowance Mix.
Performance & Succession: Rating Distribution, 9-Box, Succession Coverage.
Compliance & Discipline: Case Count, SLA Compliance, Repeated Offenses.

Dashboards Siap Pakai

  • Executive Overview — ringkasan KPI strategis lintas domain.
  • Workforce Composition — komposisi, tenure, diversity.
  • Recruitment Funnel — stage metrics dan bottleneck.

  • Performance & Succession — distribusi rating & coverage suksesi.

Installation

  1. Tambahkan addon ke addons_path Odoo.
  2. Update Apps list, cari OneHR — Analytics & KPI Dashboards.
  3. Install; dependensi akan terpasang otomatis.

Configuration

  • Atur KPI Tiles (rumus, target, warna) & susun Dashboard.
  • Aktifkan Scheduler Snapshot & tentukan periodenya.
  • Tetapkan Threshold & penerima alert.
  • Opsional: aktifkan Spreadsheet sync untuk laporan dinamis.

Security

  • Groups: HR Analyst (read), HR Manager (manage), HR Admin (full).
  • Record Rules: pembatasan data per company/branch.
  • PII awareness: akses ke data sensitif mengikuti modul keamanan.
